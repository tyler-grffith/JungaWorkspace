I will design and develop a universal workspace environment. This is an application that can do all the things I do on the computer natively. This will facilitate a universal project library that I can design to be readily accessible and referenceable. The purpose of this product is to facilitate the **creation**, **storage**, and **presentation** of work, so another key functionality will be the ability to export things (portfolios, individual portfolio components, blog posts, websites…).

This semester I will design and develop a functional part of this. My agents and I will create native versions of two of my favorite apps which already share underlying form (spreadsheets and graphing calculator) under the same project manager shell. This will allow me to bring in the many projects of those two types into this app. This new interface should allow the user to build things from scratch that they could have built in google sheets or desmos, and it should also take projects made in those other apps and make them it’s own.
